Shaadi ka mandap, aapke bharose par

Vinay Tent House and Jaimal Stage is run by Vinay Yadav from Bealuha Bazar, Siddharthnagar. We set up the tent, the stage and the decoration for your family's biggest days, so you can spend them with your guests.

From a waterproof pandal and a flower-decked jaimal stage to dining tables, velvet chair covers and lighting, everything is planned and fitted by our own team. We visit your venue, agree the design and the budget with you, and finish the setup before your guests arrive.
